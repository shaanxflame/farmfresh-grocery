Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

What is the minimum order amount?

The minimum order amount is ₹99. We have no delivery charges for orders above ₹299. For orders below ₹299, a nominal delivery charge of ₹20 applies.

What are your delivery timings?

We deliver from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM daily, including Sundays and holidays. Same-day delivery is available for orders placed before 7:00 PM.

What if I receive a damaged or rotten item?

We have a 100% quality guarantee. If any item is damaged, rotten, or unsatisfactory, we'll either replace it immediately or provide a full refund. Just notify us within 2 hours of delivery.

Do you accept online payments?

Yes! We accept UPI payments (PhonePe, GPay, PayTM) and cash on delivery. Online payment links are shared via WhatsApp for your convenience.

Can I schedule delivery for a specific time?

Absolutely! You can schedule delivery for a specific time slot (morning, afternoon, or evening) when placing your order via WhatsApp or phone call.
