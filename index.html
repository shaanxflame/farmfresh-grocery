Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our service

How do I place an order?

Simply send us a message on WhatsApp with your order list including items, quantities, and your delivery address. We'll confirm availability and provide the total amount.

What are your delivery timings?

We deliver from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM every day, including Sundays and holidays. Orders placed before 8:00 PM will be delivered the same day.

Do you accept cash on delivery?

Yes, we accept cash on delivery for all orders. We also accept UPI payments for contactless transactions.

What if I receive a damaged or spoiled item?

We offer a 100% satisfaction guarantee. If you receive any damaged or spoiled items, simply message us on WhatsApp with a photo, and we'll provide an immediate refund or replacement.
