Our Mission & Vision

Why we do what we do

Our Mission

To make fresh, nutritious food accessible to every household in Silchar while supporting local farmers and promoting sustainable agriculture practices.

Our Vision

To become Silchar's most trusted farm-to-home delivery service, setting new standards for freshness, quality, and customer satisfaction.

Our Promise

Every product we deliver is handpicked, quality-checked, and guaranteed fresh. Your satisfaction is our top priority.

Our Journey

From a simple idea to Silchar's favorite fresh grocery service

Fresh Farm began with a simple observation: families in Silchar were spending too much time traveling to markets for fresh vegetables, often compromising on quality and freshness. We saw an opportunity to bridge the gap between local farms and urban households.

Starting with just three farmers and a WhatsApp number, we delivered our first order in 2023. The response was overwhelming — families loved the convenience, the freshness, and the personal touch.

Today, we work with over 50 local farmers and have delivered more than 2,000 orders, but our core values remain unchanged: freshness first, customer trust, and supporting local agriculture.
